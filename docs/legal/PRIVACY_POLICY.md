# Privacy Policy for Classlly

**Last Updated:** January 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Classlly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, including:
*   **Personal Data:** Name, email address, and profile picture (if provided via Google/Apple Sign-In).
*   **User Content:** Notes, tasks, course information, and images you upload or create within the App.
*   **Usage Data:** Anonymized data regarding your interaction with the App features (e.g., study time).

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
*   Create and manage your account.
*   Sync your data across devices.
*   Provide customer support.
*   Improve the App's functionality and user experience.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored securely using Supabase. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party services for authentication (Google, Apple) and data storage (Supabase). These services have their own privacy policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy
We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 13.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@classlly.com
